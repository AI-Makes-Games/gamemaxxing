# When a student is ahead

Story games and quick builders can be hours ahead of the plan. Playtests need another person, so don't rush a student into one early. Offer **two or three** of these, let them pick one, and use the `build` or `art` skill to do it (one small change at a time, tested). Keep an eye on the clock: when the next playtest time comes, stop and move on.

## Any template

- **A better ending:** add a third ending, or a short scene before the end that pays off their choice.
- **More words:** a new character with one conversation, written in their own words (the scene skill's three questions work well here).
- **Polish the title screen:** title, tagline and how-to-play line; make a logo with the art skill.
- **More art:** props, a second character, or a "try again" on the hero (if credits allow).
- **Their later list:** anything in `design/later.md` that fits in 20 minutes.

## Adventure

- A second room, joined to the first by a door.
- A secret: a hidden item that changes an ending.

## Platformer

- More level after the current end, with a new kind of jump.
- More collectibles placed so they're hard to reach.

## Story

- A background picture for every scene (the art skill; about 12 credits each).
- A scene where a choice made earlier comes back.
- Turn on the countdown for one big choice (`timed_choices` in `game.json`, `timer` on the scene).

Write anything they want but don't have time for in `design/later.md`, so they can keep going after the workshop.
